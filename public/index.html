RA severity grading from hand X-rays

Upload a hand or wrist radiograph. The model finds the joints, crops the hand, grades rheumatoid arthritis severity as Mild, Moderate or Severe, and shows where it looked. Uncertain cases are deferred to a clinician instead of being given a forced grade.

Research prototype, not a medical device. The grade must be confirmed by a clinician. The model was trained on rheumatoid arthritis images only, so it does not detect normal hands.
